# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Club managers registering clubs, teams and players for TMS Taekwondo tournaments, often at a desk during registration week, and parents and players checking something quickly on a phone, sometimes in a bright, noisy sports hall on competition day. They write in English or Bahasa Malaysia. They want one clear, correct answer fast, or to know exactly who to contact.

## Product Purpose

The TMS Help Centre assistant answers questions about club, team and player registration, payments, accounts, draws, schedules and results, using only TMS's official guides (a Dify knowledge base plus answers written and approved by TMS staff). Success: a correct answer in seconds; when the assistant can't help, the visitor is handed to TMS staff with the right contact details instead of a guess.

## Brand Personality

Calm, official, trustworthy. Plain-spoken and brief. Confidence comes from showing where an answer comes from (verified by TMS, the guides it used), not from decoration.

## Anti-references

- The flashy "AI chatbot" look: gradients, glowing orbs, glass panels, sparkle overload.
- Consumer messaging apps: emoji, coloured bubbles everywhere, typing theatrics.
- SaaS landing-page clichés: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids.
- Busy sports-ticketing sites with banners competing for attention.

## Design Principles

1. The answer is the interface: content first, chrome recedes.
2. Show provenance at a glance: verified, AI-from-documents and hand-off must never look alike.
3. Never strand the visitor: whenever the assistant can't help, the way to a person is right there.
4. Bilingual by default: English and Bahasa Malaysia are equals, and layouts survive the longer Malay strings.
5. Phone and desktop are equal citizens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Fully keyboard operable with visible focus. New answers announced to screen readers. Reduced-motion respected. Contrast that holds up under bright venue lighting. Touch targets of at least 44px.
